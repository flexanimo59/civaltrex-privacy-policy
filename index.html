This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app CIVALTREX Calculator (package name com.civaltrex.app, "CIVALTREX", "the app") handles information. The app is published by CIVALTREX Labs ("we", "us").

In short: your calculations, projects and history stay on your device. CIVALTREX has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not send your calculation data to us. The app is free and shows ads from Google; the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects some device and usage information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described below.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the CIVALTREX Calculator app for Android, distributed through Google Play. It does not apply to other apps, websites or services, including Google's, which have their own privacy policies.

2. Information the app stores on your device

CIVALTREX is designed to work offline. The following information is created by you and stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Calculations: the values you enter, the results, and the calculation history (the app keeps your last 500 calculations that are not saved in a project).
  • Projects: project names and the optional details you type, such as client or site, location and notes, and the calculations you save in them.
  • Favorites and recent calculators, and the last values entered in each calculator.
  • Settings: theme, unit system, app language and whether you have completed the first-launch introduction.
  • PDF export balance: how many free PDF exports are available and the date of the last daily export.

We do not receive this information. It is not sent to us or to any server by CIVALTREX. You decide what to type into free-text fields such as project names, client, location and notes; please avoid entering information you do not want stored on your device.

3. Device backup and device transfer

If you have turned on Android backup for your device, Android may include the app's calculation database and settings in your device backup (for example in your Google account) and may restore them on a new device. This is controlled by Android and your device settings, not by us. Advertising and consent data and generated PDF files are not included in the app's backup rules.

4. PDF reports and sharing

When you create a PDF report, it is generated on your device and stored temporarily in the app's private cache. Temporary PDF files are deleted automatically after 24 hours. A PDF or a text summary leaves the app only when you choose to share it or save it: you pick the app or location (for example an email app, a messaging app or a folder) through Android's share sheet or file picker. That app or service then handles the file under its own privacy policy. CIVALTREX does not upload PDF reports anywhere.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CIVALTREX is free and is supported by ads. The app shows a banner ad and, only when you choose to watch one, a rewarded video ad that unlocks a PDF export. Ads are provided by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

According to Google's published disclosure for this SDK, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit using TLS. This data is collected by Google, not by us; we donot receive your advertising ID or IP address from the SDK. Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Ads require an internet connection. The calculators themselves do not.

6. Consent and your advertising choices

Where the law requires it — for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherapplicable under US state privacy laws — the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent or show your choices before ads use your data. On the first launch, this message aapp checks the status of your choices with Google each time itsave your choices on your device.

You can:

  • Review or change your consent choices at any time in where this option is required in your region.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Google → Ads, or Security & privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Decline personalized ads in the consent message where it is offered. If you do not give consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads as described in Google's policies.

7. Permissions

CIVALTREX does not ask you for any runtime permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The installed app declares the following permissions, which Android grants automatically and which are added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Android libraries:

  • Internet access and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): needed to request and show ads and to check consent status.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the Google Mob SDK read the Android advertising ID.
  • Basic phone state (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE): a non-dangerous permission declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK that allows read-only access to basic information such as the cellular network type asoftware version. It does not give access to phone numbers or calls.
  • Wake lock and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): declared by Android's WorkManager library, which is included through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for background tasks. CIVALTREX itself does not run foreground services.
  • An Android library permission limited to the app itself (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION).

8. Analytics and crash reporting

CIVALTREX does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service, and we do not track calculators. The diagnostic and interaction data described in section 5 is collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

9. How information is shared

We do not sell your information and we do not share your calculation or project data with anyone, because we doreceive it. Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is collected and processed by Google as described insection 5. Files and text that you choose to share are sent to the app or service you select.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual calculations, clear the history, delete projects, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Temporary PDF files are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Device backups are kept according to your Android backup settings and your backup provider.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is retained and deleted according to Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Because we do not hold your data on any server, we cannot access, export or delete it for you; you remain in control of the data on your device.

11. Security

Your app data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; please protect your device with a screen lock and keep it updated.

12. Children's privacy

CIVALTREX is a professional tool for construction and engineering work and is intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed to children, and it is not designed for children under 13 or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. International users

CIVALTREX can be used worldwide. Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be processed by Google in countries other than your own, as described in Google's privacy policy. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under local data protection laws, such as the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your data, most of these requests concern data on your own device, which you control, or data held by Google, which you can manage through Google's tools and policies. You can also contact us with questions.

14. Your choices at a glance

  • Use the calculators without an internet connection.
  • Change ad consent choices in Settings → Privacy options (where required).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Choose whether and where to share PDF reports and results.
  • Delete calculations, history and projects in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
